The Hub on 7th is the nicest building I've lived in, but you pay for it — my one-bedroom was $1,150/month. It's brand new so everything works and the downtown location is unbeatable, restaurants and bars right outside. Downside: weekends are LOUD. If you go to bed early, this is not your place. Management is actually responsive though, they fixed my dishwasher within a day. Front desk holds your packages which is nice.
