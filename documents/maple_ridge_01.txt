Lived at Maple Ridge for two years and honestly the amenities are the only reason I stayed. The gym and pool are genuinely nice and it's a quick drive to campus. BUT — management is slow on maintenance. My AC went out in August and it took them 9 days to fix it. Walls are thin too, I could hear my neighbor's TV every night. Rent was about $600/month for my room in a 3-bedroom. Got most of my deposit back, only lost $50 for "cleaning."
