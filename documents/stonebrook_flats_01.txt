Do NOT recommend Stonebrook Flats if you care about parking. It's right next to campus which is amazing for walking to class, but the parking lot is way too small and I got my car towed twice. The in-unit washer/dryer is clutch though, no more hauling laundry. Found mold in the bathroom ceiling within the first month and management blamed me for "not running the fan." Took three emails to get it cleaned. Walls are okay, didn't hear neighbors much.
