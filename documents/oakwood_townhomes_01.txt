Oakwood Townhomes is great if you have a group of friends. We did a 4-bedroom with an attached garage and it felt like an actual house. Super quiet neighborhood, no party noise. The catch: they lock you into a 12-month lease with no summer sublet option, so you pay through the summer even if you go home. Also we never got our full deposit back — they kept $400 claiming carpet damage that was already there when we moved in. Take move-in photos!!
