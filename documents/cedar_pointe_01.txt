Cedar Pointe is cheap for a reason — it's far from campus, like a 20 minute drive, so you NEED a car. Rent was only $475 which is why I put up with it. The big problem is heating. In January my apartment never got above 62 degrees no matter what I set the thermostat to, and maintenance just said "that's normal for the older units." It is not normal. Parking is free and easy at least.
